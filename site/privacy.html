Privacy policy.

Updated 6 November 2026.

Your project enquiry

We use the name, email address, selected service and project description you provide to respond to your enquiry. Phone, business, budget and timeline are optional. Please avoid including sensitive personal information.

Delivery and storage

Enquiries are validated by this website and delivered through Web3Forms, or an email provider configured by the studio. The form shares your submitted details with that provider for delivery. This website does not maintain a separate enquiry database. Correspondence may remain in the studio’s email inbox for handling the enquiry and any resulting project.

Site assets and analytics

Fonts and animation assets are served locally. This version does not load advertising or analytics trackers. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request logs to serve and protect the site.

Your choices

You can ask about or request deletion of information you supplied by contacting thebenzzcreationsofficial@gmail.com. You can also contact the studio directly instead of using the form.
